Derryth Nash is an emerging artist and singer/song writer, whose work blends her love of music with her deep connection to nature.

Her modern acrylic paintings re-imagine birds through bold colour and rhythmic composition, capturing the movement and musicality she finds in nature, or depict her love of the human form revealing organic shapes, shifting light, and subtle gestures. Guided by a distinctive style that balances softness with strength, Derryth’s canvases invite viewers into moments of stillness and connection.

Derryth has expanded her practice into three dimensions with a series of intimate sculptures crafted from recycled guitar strings, feathers and other materials. This work also captures her passion for sustainability and explores themes of home, fragility, and resilience in her nest‑like forms. Together, her work offers a unified vision of nature reimagined through her thoughtful, contemporary lens. See some of her avilable work below.
