Derryth Nash is an emerging artist and singer/songwriter whose work blends her love of music with her deep connection to nature.

Her modern acrylic paintings re‑imagine birds through bold colour and rhythmic composition, capturing movement, musicality, and the emotional resonance she finds in nature. Her figurative works explore organic shapes, shifting light, and subtle gestures, balancing softness with strength.

Derryth has expanded her practice into three dimensions with intimate sculptures crafted from recycled guitar strings, feathers, and other materials. These nest‑like forms explore themes of home, fragility, and resilience.

Together, her work offers a unified vision of nature reimagined through a thoughtful, contemporary lens. See some of her available work below.
