Daily Whispers was inspired from living in the daily grind of a world where mental health is on the decline, social isolation is on the increase and human connection is in need of a reboot.

If you know someone going through a challenging time, or just want to remind someone how special they are to you, Daily Whipsers sends a quote from you, to them daily to remind them they are imortant.
