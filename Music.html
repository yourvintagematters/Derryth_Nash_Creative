Derryth has been involved with music since she was a child. From touring internationally with choirs, to musical theatre and gigging in various rgroups around Canberra and surrounds, music is in her DNA.

She has recently started facilitating singing‑for‑wellness sessions, focusing on gentle vocal practices, breathwork, and accessible group singing that support emotional wellbeing and human connection.

Singing for Wellness sessions

"When we sing, the body remembers how to relax and the heart remembers how to open."

Singing‑for‑wellness sessions offer a gentle, uplifting space to breathe, connect, and feel good through simple vocal practices. These sessions blend easy group singing, calming breathwork, and supportive community energy to help people relax, boost mood, and rediscover joy. No experience needed — just a willingness to show up, soften, and let your voice bring you back to yourself.
