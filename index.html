Derryth Nash is a purpose‑driven creator, facilitator, and musician who believes deeply in human connection, community, and the simple things in life. With a background in Health Education and extensive experience supporting people across the Mental Health, Disability, and Aged Care sectors, she brings an enthusiastic and human‑centred approach to everything she does. Her work is grounded in kindness, creativity, and a passion and commitment to be part of the solution in a fast, modern world that often forgets what really matters.
